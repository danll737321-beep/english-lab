# English Lab

California high school English (Grades 9–12): vocabulary, grammar and classic novel guides.

- **Grade 9 (complete):** 360 words in 36 units, 8 grammar topics with practice, and guides for *To Kill a Mockingbird*, *Romeo and Juliet*, *The Odyssey* and *Of Mice and Men*.
- **Grades 10–12:** planned.
- Two interfaces: English only (`#en`) and English + Traditional Chinese key terms (`#zh-tw`).

Aligned to the California Common Core State Standards for ELA. Novel guides are original summaries and analysis.

Live: https://danll737321-beep.github.io/english-lab/
