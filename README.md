# English Lab

California high school English, Grades 9–12: vocabulary, grammar and classic novel guides, aligned to the California Common Core State Standards for ELA.

| Grade | Vocabulary | Grammar & language | Novels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 360 words · 36 units | 8 topics (L.9-10) | To Kill a Mockingbird · Romeo and Juliet · The Odyssey · Of Mice and Men |
| 10 | 360 words · 36 units | 8 topics (L/W.9-10) | Lord of the Flies · Night · Fahrenheit 451 · Macbeth |
| 11 | 360 words · 36 units | 8 topics (L.11-12) | The Great Gatsby · The Crucible · Adventures of Huckleberry Finn · Their Eyes Were Watching God |
| 12 | 360 words · 36 units | 8 topics (L/W.11-12) | Hamlet · 1984 · Frankenstein · Things Fall Apart |

Each novel guide includes background, section summaries, characters, themes and symbols, literary devices and six claim–evidence–reasoning critical-thinking questions. Each grammar topic has a rule, examples and five practice questions.

Each grade's Novels tab also lists the district **ELA Extended Reading Guide** titles (32 books and plays, Grades 9–12) by unit, with Lexile, year, a short bilingual summary, content warnings, frontloading topics and the 360learn link. Titles that already have a full guide link to it.

Two interfaces:
- English only: `#en`
- English + Traditional Chinese support: `#zh-tw`

Novel guides are original summaries and analysis; students should read the full texts.
